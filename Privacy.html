Privacy Policy — Kamurocho Companion

Last updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Kamurocho Companion Twitch Extension ("the Extension") handles information when you use it on the Twitch channel Aldares.

1. What we collect

The Extension only handles information that is required to display your in-game stats from the Kamurocho Yakuza chat minigame:

  • Twitch User ID and username — only when you explicitly grant identity sharing through Twitch's "Share Identity" prompt. This is used solely to look up your in-game profile.
  • In-game data tied to your Twitch username — yen balance, rep, rank, cooldowns, owned businesses, fight/gamble history. This data is generated by the chat minigame on the broadcaster's stream and is not personal information beyond what is already public on Twitch chat.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your real name, email address, IP address, location, or any contact details.
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect any data from viewers who have not granted identity sharing AND have not searched for a username.
  • We do not collect data from minors knowingly. The Extension is intended for users 13+ in line with Twitch's Terms of Service.

3. How the data is used

Your Twitch username is sent to a private server operated by the broadcaster (Aldares) to look up the corresponding in-game profile. The server returns the requested stats and the Extension displays them in your browser. No data is stored remotely beyond what already exists in the broadcaster's local minigame files.

4. Local storage

The Extension uses your browser's localStorage to remember the username you last searched, so you don't have to type it again on every visit. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties. The only network requests made by the Extension are to:

  • Twitch's own servers (handled automatically by the Twitch Extension Helper).
  • The broadcaster's private game server, used solely to fetch your in-game stats.

6. Your rights

You can stop using the Extension at any time. You can revoke identity sharing through Twitch's settings at any time. To request deletion of your in-game data, contact the broadcaster directly via Twitch.

7. Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the broadcaster (Aldares) via Twitch direct message at twitch.tv/aldares.
